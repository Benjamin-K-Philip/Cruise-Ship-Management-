                                        Vegan Meals
This meal is available for passengers requiring a western style vegan‑vegetarian
meal.
It can contain all types of vegetables, fruits, legumes, grains, cereals, soy products,
nuts, seeds and vegan‑suitable animal alternatives.
It does NOT contain any animal product or by‑product such as meat, poultry, fish, seafood,
eggs, dairy, animal rennet, animal gelatin, animal fat and honey.
